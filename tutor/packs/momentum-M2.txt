EXAM TRAP: The minus sign that went missing (Built on IEB Nov 2024, Paper 1, Question 5.2.2.)
- Question: Trolley A (2 kg) moves at 3 m·s⁻¹ to the right. Trolley B (1 kg) moves at 2 m·s⁻¹ to the left. They collide on a frictionless track, and A stops. Calculate the velocity of B after the collision. (4)
- The usual wrong answer: (2)(3) + (1)(2) = (2)(0) + (1)v, so v = 8 m·s⁻¹
- What went wrong: B was moving left, so its velocity is −2 m·s⁻¹ with right as positive. Momentum is a vector: the sign carries the direction.
- The memo: Right positive. Σp before = Σp after: (2)(3) + (1)(−2) = (2)(0) + (1)vB, so 4 = vB. vB = 4,00 m·s⁻¹ to the right.
- The examiners, word for word: "Candidates must remember that momentum is a vector quantity and use of signs to deal with momentum in opposite directions is important." (IEB Examiners' Report 2017, Paper 1, Question 5)
