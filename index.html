About

Who is Mr R?

I'm Noel Robert. I have taught Physics and Chemistry in secondary schools for more than twenty years, nineteen of them at Hilton College. I teach IEB Physical Sciences from Grade 10 to 12, and I have also taught Cambridge IGCSE Physics and AS and A Level Chemistry. I now coordinate IEB Physical Sciences at Reddam House Durbanville.

I did IEB national examination work from 2009 to 2022. For most of my career I have also worked alongside other science teachers: as a mentor with the VULA outreach programme in under-resourced schools, and as the lead of a teacher intern programme. Outside the lab, I spent many years leading outdoor journeys and expeditions.

In my classroom, learners work with a pen and paper, not behind a screen. Science marks are earned by writing each step out until it becomes muscle memory. This site is for home. The Coursebook and its Answer book, the Watch, Train and Check steps in the app, and the Studios where you can see it happen all reinforce what was done in class. They do not replace it.

Everything here started as something my own learners needed. It is here for any learner who wants to get better at science, and for any teacher who can use it.
