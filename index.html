About

Who is Mr R?

I'm Noel Robert. I teach Physical Sciences to Grades 10 to 12, and I write the textbooks my classes use. Everything on this site started as something my own learners needed.

It is here for any learner who wants to get better at science, and for any teacher who can use it.
