Module 1 · The laboratory

MODULE 1
The laboratory
Print packs 01, 02 and 03a
📖 Coursebook pp 7–34 coursebook pages · not in this preview
🖨️ Pack 01 coursebook pages · not in this preview🖨️ Pack 02 coursebook pages · not in this preview🧪 Pack 03a (practicals) coursebook pages · not in this preview🗂️ Which pack when coursebook pages · not in this preview
You must be able to
  • Follow the laboratory safety rules and say which rule is broken in a story
  • Name the hazard symbols and say what each one warns you about
  • Name common apparatus, say what each is used for and choose the right apparatus for a task
  • Read a measuring cylinder at eye level from the bottom of the meniscus; know that 1 mL = 1 cm³
  • Find the volume of an irregular solid by displacement (final reading − initial reading)
  • Label a Bunsen burner, describe the safety and heating flames and light it safely in the correct order
