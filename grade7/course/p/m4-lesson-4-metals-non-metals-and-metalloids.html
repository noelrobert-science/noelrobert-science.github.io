Metalloids · on the line

⚠️ Hydrogen is a non-metal: a colourless gas.
Definitions to learn (p 112)

Non-metals: Brittle (as solids). Do not conduct electricity.

Metalloids: Metalloids share properties with both metals and non-metals.

Metalloids: boron, silicon, germanium, arsenic, antimony, tellurium. Silicon and germanium are semiconductors: computer chips and solar panels.
