Groups and periods
The columns of the periodic table are called groups and the rows are called periods.

💡 Count periods from the top: hydrogen and helium are Period 1.
Group numbers in this book (Coursebook pages 86–87)

This book uses Group 1 to Group 7 for the main groups, as the tests do, and Group 8 for the noble gases (the exam table prints VIII; you may also see 18 or 0). The transition metals in the middle have no Grade 7 group number.
