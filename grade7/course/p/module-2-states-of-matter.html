Module 2 · States of matter

MODULE 2
States of matter
Print pack 04
📖 Coursebook pp 35–67 coursebook pages · not in this preview
🖨️ Pack 04 coursebook pages · not in this preview
You must be able to
  • Describe matter and the four ideas of the particle model
  • Describe the particles in solids, liquids and gases, and draw their particle pictures
  • Name the six changes of state and say whether energy is taken in or given out
  • Give two differences between boiling and evaporation
  • Use melting and boiling points to say whether a substance is a solid, a liquid or a gas
  • Describe diffusion in gases and liquids
  • Tell a physical change from a chemical change, and name the reactants and products in a word equation
