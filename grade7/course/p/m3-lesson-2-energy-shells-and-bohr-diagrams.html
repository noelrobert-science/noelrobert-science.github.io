Definition to learn: the 2-8-8 rule (Coursebook page 65)

The 2-8-8 rule: The 1st energy level holds a maximum of 2 electrons, the 2nd a maximum of 8 and the 3rd a maximum of 8. Fill an energy level to capacity before moving to the next energy level.

Drawing a Bohr diagram (Coursebook page 67)
  1. Find the number of electrons. Look at the Periodic Table: the smaller number is the atomic (proton) number, and number of protons = number of electrons.
  2. Draw a circle and write the element symbol in it, e.g. Al. This is the nucleus.
  3. Allocate the electrons to the shells by the 2-8-8 rule. Draw a circle for each shell you need. Put the electrons in as dots, one at a time, spaced evenly.
