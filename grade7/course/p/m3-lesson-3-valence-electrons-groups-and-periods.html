Definition to learn: valence electrons (p 91)

Valence electrons: Electrons that are found on the outermost energy level. The number of valence electrons determines the chemical properties of an element.

Shells, groups and periods (p 92)

The number of shells is the period number. For Groups 1 to 7 the number of valence electrons is the group number. The noble gases (Group 8) have a full outer shell.

Which atom is it? (p 93)
  1. Count all the electrons. In a neutral atom, electrons = protons = atomic number.
  2. Find that atomic number on the Periodic Table: that is the element.
  3. Check: the number of shells gives the period and the outer electrons give the group.
  4. Metal or non-metal? Left of the zigzag line = metal, right = non-metal.
