🍋 Acids and bases · Coursebook pages 116–119
Acids
Acids taste sour. Strong acids are corrosive and can ‘eat away’ at materials like metal, stone and flesh. Acids produce hydrogen ions (H+) when dissolved in water.
Bases
Bases feel soapy and taste bitter. Like acids, they can ‘burn’ the skin.
Alkali
Bases that can dissolve in water (known as soluble bases).
⚠️ Never taste or feel a chemical to test it.
Definitions to learn (Coursebook pages 116–119)

Acids: Acids taste sour. Strong acids are corrosive and can ‘eat away’ at materials like metal, stone and flesh. Acids produce hydrogen ions (H+) when dissolved in water.

Bases: Bases feel soapy and taste bitter. Like acids, they can ‘burn’ the skin.

Alkali: Bases that can dissolve in water (known as soluble bases).

Acids in the laboratory (Coursebook page 118)

hydrochloric acid: in your stomach · sulfuric acid: in car batteries · nitric acid: used to make fertilisers · ethanoic acid: in vinegar
