Learn how to learn

GRADE 7 CHEMISTRY
🧠 Learn how to learn
Three habits that make it stick, in this subject and every other one.
Your brain gets stronger the way muscles do: by working.
Reading something again feels good, but it doesn't stick. These three habits do.
1Test yourself ✏️
Cover it. Say it or write it. Then check.
Pulling it out of your memory is what puts it there.
Look for: Ready? Quick check Check-ins
2Come back later 📅
Try it again a week later. Write the date in Try again on.
If it feels hard, that's your brain making it stick.
Look for: Look back Try again on ___
3Copy, then try 🔁
Follow the worked example line by line. Then do the next one on your own.
Watching isn't learning. Doing is.
Look for: Worked example → Now you
Why check-ins count 0 %
They're practice on purpose. Testing yourself is how you learn, not just how we measure. Get it wrong here, fix it, and it won't trip you up in the test.
These habits work everywhere: History dates, French words, Maths methods. Learn them once and use them for life.
