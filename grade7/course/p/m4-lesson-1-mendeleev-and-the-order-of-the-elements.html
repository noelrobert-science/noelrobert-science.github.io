🔢 Today: atomic number
The Periodic Table
The elements are arranged in increasing order of their atomic number.
Mendeleev (p 106)

In 1869 Dmitri Mendeleev wrote each element and its properties on a card, and arranged the cards in order of the mass of their atoms.

He put elements that behave alike in the same column, even when that meant leaving gaps. He said the gaps were for elements nobody had found yet, and predicted their properties.

Today the elements are arranged in order of their atomic number (the number of protons), not their mass.
