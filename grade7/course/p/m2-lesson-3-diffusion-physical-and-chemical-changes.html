🟤 Diffusion · p 57
Diffusion
Diffusion is the movement of particles from a higher concentration to a lower concentration.
Gases: fast Liquids: slower Warmer: faster
⚗️ Physical or chemical change? · p 59
Physical change
no new substance; the substance stays the same.
Chemical change
one or more new substances form.
Signs of a chemical change (p 59)
  • a gas fizzes off
  • a colour change
  • a solid (precipitate) appears
  • it gets hot or cold
  • light is given out

Chemical reaction: During a chemical reaction, the atoms in the reactants rearrange to form products.
