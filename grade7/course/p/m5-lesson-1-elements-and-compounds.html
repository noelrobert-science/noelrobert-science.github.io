Element

🔗 Compounds: different atoms bonded · p 134
Compound
A substance in which two or more different elements are chemically bonded together.
Definitions to learn (pp 132 to 134)

Element: A substance composed of a single type of atom. It cannot be broken down into another substance by chemical or physical means.

Compound: A substance in which two or more different elements are chemically bonded together.

Molecules and particle pictures (pp 132 to 133)

A group of two or more atoms that are chemically bonded (joined) together is called a molecule.

In particle pictures, atoms that touch are bonded together into one particle. Atoms that are apart are not bonded. Count the particles, not the atoms.

Symbols from Latin (p 133)

Write the second letter small: Co is cobalt; CO is a compound of carbon and oxygen.

sodium Na (natrium) · potassium K (kalium) · iron Fe (ferrum) · copper Cu (cuprum) · silver Ag (argentum) · gold Au (aurum) · mercury Hg (hydrargyrum)
