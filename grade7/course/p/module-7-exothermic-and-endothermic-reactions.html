You must be able to
  • Define exothermic and endothermic reactions and say what a thermometer shows for each
  • Use the words system and surroundings
  • Calculate a temperature change (final − initial) with its sign and use it to classify a reaction
  • Give examples of exothermic and endothermic reactions
  • Tell an energy change in a reaction from an energy change in a physical change (melting, evaporating, dissolving)
