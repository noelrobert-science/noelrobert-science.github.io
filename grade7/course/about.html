About this preview

This is a public preview copy of a Grade 7 Chemistry course being prepared in Canvas for 2027. The real course stays private.

  • Same pages, same order. The lesson pages, module pages and check-ins were copied from the course as it stands on 27 November 2026.
  • The videos are our own. Each one is built backwards from exactly what earns the marks, and from the coursebook section it goes with. The voices are AI voices and are labelled.
  • The check-ins work. They mark themselves in your browser. Nothing you type is saved or sent anywhere.
  • Coursebook pages are not public. Where the real course opens a coursebook or print pack, this copy shows a grey note instead.
  • No learner names, marks or messages appear anywhere in this copy.
