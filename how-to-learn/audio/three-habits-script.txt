# Three habits
Here's a secret about learning. Reading your notes again feels like learning, but most of it is gone by next week. What actually works is a little harder.

[pause 1]

First, test yourself. Cover the page and write what you remember, then check. Pulling it out of your memory is what puts it there.

[pause 1]

Second, come back later. Try it again a week from now. If it feels hard, good. That's your brain making it stick.

[pause 1]

Third, copy, then try. Follow my worked example line by line, then do the next one on your own, with your pen.

[pause 1]

And the check-ins? They count nothing on purpose. Get it wrong there, fix it, and it won't catch you in the test. These habits work in every subject. Use them for life.
