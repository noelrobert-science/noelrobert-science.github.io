Before you start

  • Sit facing a window or a lamp, with the camera at eye level. A quiet room, phone on silent, a glass of water.
  • Keep your face inside the dashed circle. That is what learners see.
  • Change any words to how you would really say them: click the text and type. Your changes are saved with the recording.
  • Lines come one at a time, just under your webcam, so your eyes stay near the lens. Press → (or click the text) for the next line, even while recording. Read the line, then look up at the camera and say it.
  • One scene at a time. Retake as often as you like; only the last take is kept.
